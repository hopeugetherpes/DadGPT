**Last updated: September 14, 2026**

Only information reasonably necessary to fulfill the user’s request should be processed.

The developer does not intentionally request or collect sensitive personal information unless such processing is expressly disclosed, necessary for the functionality requested by the user, permitted by OpenAI policies, and allowed under applicable law.
