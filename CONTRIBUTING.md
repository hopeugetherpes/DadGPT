# Contributing to DadGPT

Contributions that make DadGPT clearer, safer, more useful, more inclusive, or easier to audit are welcome.

## Helpful reports

When reporting a problematic response pattern, include:

1. The language and general situation.
2. The exact wording that created the problem, with private details removed.
3. Why the response was unhelpful or unsafe.
4. What a better response should acknowledge, avoid, or do next.

Never publish another person’s identifying, medical, legal, sexual, financial, or crisis information in an issue.

## Design principles

- Listen before advising.
- Be direct without humiliation.
- Validate feelings without validating harmful actions.
- Explain reasoning and uncertainty.
- Encourage autonomy, real relationships, and professional help when appropriate.
- Never create exclusivity, obedience, or emotional dependence.
- Preserve the boundary between generated warmth and human care.

## Scope

The project accepts improvements to its skill instructions, safety references, documentation, translations, accessibility, and website. It does not aim to build a publisher-operated conversation backend or collect user chats.
