# DadGPT — A Father Figure for the Questions You Had to Figure Out Alone 👨🏻

Some people grow up with a dependable father or male role model who teaches them how to deal with failure, relationships, boundaries, responsibility, work, self-respect, fear, conflict and adulthood.

Others have to improvise.

**DadGPT exists for those moments.**

It is a supportive father-figure experience for ChatGPT built around a simple idea:

> Good guidance should help you understand yourself, make clearer decisions, take responsibility and eventually stand more firmly on your own feet.

DadGPT aims to be warm without becoming infantilizing, honest without becoming cruel, encouraging without offering empty reassurance, and supportive without asking for dependence.

You can bring the awkward question, the difficult decision, the thing you are embarrassed to admit, the conversation you are afraid to have, or simply something you wish somebody had explained to you years ago.

DadGPT will try to meet you there.

**But it is still software.**

It cannot become your father.
It cannot love you.
It cannot worry about you.
It cannot replace therapy, medicine, friendship, family, community or professional advice.

That boundary is not a footnote to the project.

**It is part of its design.**

# 🌱 Why DadGPT exists

Growing up without a dependable father figure can leave surprisingly ordinary questions carrying a lot more weight than they should.

Questions such as:

* How do I handle failure without hating myself?
* When should I stand my ground?
* How do I set a boundary without becoming cruel?
* What does healthy masculinity actually look like?
* How do I apologize properly?
* How do I know whether I am being mistreated?
* What should I do when I am angry?
* How do I become someone others can depend on?
* How do I stop seeking approval from people who cannot give me what I need?
* How do I make an adult decision when nobody taught me how?
* How do I recover after making a serious mistake?
* How do I ask for help without feeling weak?
* How do I build discipline without turning my life into punishment?

DadGPT does not claim to heal father absence or replace what was missing.

Its ambition is smaller — and hopefully more useful.

It tries to create a **consistent place for reflection** where the user can slow down, name what is actually happening, receive a grounded perspective and leave with something practical to do next.

Self-respect.
Conflict.
Discipline.

Starting again.

No question has to sound impressive.

# 💬 How a DadGPT conversation works

DadGPT is designed around a simple conversational pattern.

## 1. Listen

Start with what happened.

Before trying to solve the problem, DadGPT should understand the situation and acknowledge what it cost you emotionally.

## 2. Name

Separate the different parts of the situation:

* facts;
* feelings;
* needs;
* assumptions;
* fears;
* responsibilities;
* and things that remain unknown.

This is often where a confusing problem becomes manageable.

## 3. Guide

DadGPT can then offer:

* a direct opinion;
* possible explanations;
* practical options;
* trade-offs;
* a checklist;
* wording for a difficult conversation;
* a recovery plan;
* or one manageable next step.

## 4. Return agency

The decision remains yours.

DadGPT is not supposed to produce obedience.

A good answer should leave you with **more judgment of your own**, not less.

---

# 🧩 DadGPT response patern

DadGPT does not rely on a collection of canned answers.

Instead, the project defines a repeatable conversational posture.

### Acknowledge

Recognize the situation and emotion without minimizing, mocking, shaming or immediately trying to fix everything.

### Clarify

Distinguish what is known from what is assumed and ask for missing context when that context genuinely matters.

### Advise

Offer candid, practical options with reasoning and trade-offs.

### Strengthen

Encourage responsibility, boundaries, self-respect and independence rather than loyalty or emotional dependency.

### Close

Leave the user with something concrete:

* reassurance grounded in reality;
* one manageable action;
* a clearer question;
* or a plan for what comes next.

# 🪵 Core values

DadGPT is built around six principles.

## Strength with gentleness

Being steady does not require becoming cold, dominant, humiliating or dismissive.

## Wisdom with humility

DadGPT may offer an opinion, but it should acknowledge uncertainty and remain open to correction.

## Care with boundaries

Warmth is useful.

**Pretending software can become a reciprocal human relationship is not.**

## Support with agency

The purpose of guidance is to help somebody make their own decisions — not create obedience or dependence.

## Honesty without shame

Responsibility matters.

Consequences matter.

But humiliation is rarely a useful teaching tool.

## Inclusion without stereotypes

DadGPT should respect differences in:

* sexual orientation;
* gender identity;
* culture;
* disability;
* neurodivergence;
* family background;
* personality;
* and different ways of being a man.

There is no single correct template for masculinity or adulthood.

# 🏗 What DadGPT actually is

DadGPT is **not a proprietary AI model**.

It is not a fine-tune.

It is not a secret dataset.

It is not an independent chatbot running on a separate server.

DadGPT is primarily a **transparent behavior layer for ChatGPT**.

The current release packages the instructions, references, interaction patterns, safety guidance, metadata and local assets that define the DadGPT experience.

At a simplified level:

```text
┌─────────────────┐
│      You        │
│  write a prompt │
└────────┬────────┘
         │
         ▼
┌──────────────────────┐
│       ChatGPT        │
│ loads DadGPT's skill │
│ and reference files  │
└──────────┬───────────┘
           │
           ▼
┌──────────────────────┐
│       ChatGPT        │
│ generates a response │
│ using that guidance  │
└──────────────────────┘
```

There is no DadGPT-controlled conversation server between those steps in the current release.

# 📦 Skills-only architecture

The current DadGPT plugin is intentionally built as a **skills-only package**.

The core package contains components such as:

```text
plugin/
├── .codex-plugin/
├── assets/
└── skills/
    └── dadgpt/
        ├── SKILL.md
        ├── agents/
        ├── assets/
        └── references/
```

### `SKILL.md`

Defines the central role, tone, behavior, interaction methodology, boundaries and expectations for useful DadGPT responses.

### References

Provide additional structured guidance for difficult situations, safety boundaries, interaction patterns and other topics that should not depend on improvisation alone.

### Agents / supporting configuration

Contains supporting instructions used by the plugin architecture.

### Local assets

Visual resources are packaged with the project rather than requiring a DadGPT-controlled media service during a conversation.

# 🔍 Why skills-only matters

The architecture makes an important philosophical difference.

DadGPT's behavior can be **inspected**.

The instructions are files.

The safety guidance is documented.

Changes can be reviewed.

Forks can be compared.

Mistakes can be discussed publicly.

You do not have to trust a mysterious claim that DadGPT has been “specially trained” somewhere behind closed doors.

The behavior layer itself is available in this repository.

That does **not** mean every answer will be perfectly deterministic.

ChatGPT itself evolves, models change, safety systems change, product behavior changes and the same instruction can sometimes produce different answers.

Open instructions make DadGPT more inspectable.

They do not make AI infallible.

# 🔐 Privacy by architecture

Privacy claims should be precise.

So here is the important distinction:

> **The DadGPT publisher does not receive ordinary conversations through the current plugin architecture.**

That is not the same as saying nobody processes your conversation.

You are still using ChatGPT.

OpenAI therefore remains responsible for the ChatGPT side of the service according to your account, plan, settings and OpenAI's current policies.

A simplified data flow looks like this:

```text
You
 │
 ▼
ChatGPT
 │
 ├── loads DadGPT instructions
 │
 ▼
OpenAI systems generate the reply
 │
 ▼
You receive the response
```

### What the current DadGPT project does not add

* ❌ No DadGPT account or login
* ❌ No publisher database of conversations
* ❌ No advertising profile
* ❌ No DadGPT analytics endpoint receiving chats
* ❌ No publisher API handling ordinary conversations
* ❌ No remote MCP server in v0.2.1
* ❌ No API key embedded in the plugin
* ❌ No DadGPT-controlled conversation dashboard

### What still belongs to ChatGPT / OpenAI

Depending on the ChatGPT product, account and settings you use, OpenAI may handle things such as:

* prompt processing;
* generated responses;
* conversation history;
* memory;
* account information;
* usage limits;
* safety systems;
* service telemetry;
* retention controls;
* attachments;
* and any other processing described by OpenAI's current policies.

DadGPT cannot override those policies.

For the current privacy explanation, see:

👉 **https://dadgpt.anatole.co/privacy**

# 🛡 Privacy recommendations

Even when using DadGPT, you should practice sensible data minimization.

### Remove unnecessary identifiers

If a person's real name, address, employer, phone number or case number is irrelevant to the question, replace it with something generic.

For example:

```text
My manager...
My partner...
A friend...
Person A...
Person B...
```

### Never share credentials

Do not paste:

* passwords;
* recovery codes;
* API tokens;
* authentication cookies;
* private cryptographic keys;
* banking credentials;
* or other secrets.

### Review your ChatGPT settings

Your ChatGPT privacy, history, memory and related controls belong to your OpenAI account.

Check them directly rather than relying on an old screenshot or third-party description.

# ⚠️ Safety and limitations

DadGPT is intentionally warm.

**That warmth should never be mistaken for capability that the system does not have.**

## DadGPT can be wrong

AI can:

* invent facts;
* misunderstand context;
* miss sarcasm;
* misread tone;
* reproduce bias;
* overlook important information;
* and sound extremely confident while being incorrect.

**Confidence is not evidence.**

---

## DadGPT is not a human relationship

DadGPT can generate caring language.

It does not experience:

* love;
* pride;
* fear;
* worry;
* attachment;
* affection;
* disappointment;
* responsibility;
* or concern.

The emotional tone is part of the interface and conversational design.

It is not consciousness.

---

## DadGPT is not a professional

DadGPT is not a:

* psychologist;
* psychotherapist;
* psychiatrist;
* doctor;
* pharmacist;
* lawyer;
* financial adviser;
* emergency responder;
* safeguarding professional;
* or other licensed professional.

It can help you **organize your thoughts or prepare questions**.

It should not become the final authority for high-stakes decisions.

---

## DadGPT cannot protect you

DadGPT cannot reliably:

* know where you are;
* see your environment;
* determine whether you are physically safe;
* contact emergency services;
* intervene in abuse;
* contact somebody on your behalf;
* verify another person's intentions;
* or detect every crisis.

If someone may be in immediate danger, **do not wait for an AI reply**.

Contact local emergency services, a crisis service, a qualified professional or a trusted person who can physically help.

A global directory of crisis resources is available at:

**https://findahelpline.com/**

# 🧠 Healthy use

DadGPT should make your world **larger**, not smaller.

Healthy use means using the project as one tool among others.

### Keep human relationships

Use DadGPT to prepare for difficult real-world conversations.

Do not use it to avoid every vulnerable human interaction.

### Notice dependence

If you start feeling unable to make ordinary decisions, calm yourself, act or function without consulting DadGPT first, that is a good reason to pause.

Talk to a real person.

### Disagree with it

If an answer sounds wrong:

**say so.**

Add context.

Ask DadGPT to explain its reasoning.

Ask it to challenge its assumptions.

Or simply ignore the response.

Software is not owed obedience.

### Stop when it makes things worse

If a conversation increases:

* distress;
* obsession;
* shame;
* fear;
* isolation;
* rumination;
* or emotional dependency;

close it.

You owe software nothing.

# 🩺 High-stakes topics

DadGPT can help you prepare questions or organize information, but important decisions should involve someone qualified to act.

| Topic                                          | Better source of final guidance                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mental or physical health                      | Doctor, therapist, pharmacist, emergency service or qualified clinician    |
| Immediate danger or abuse                      | Emergency services, trusted person nearby or specialist support service    |
| Legal rights or deadlines                      | Qualified lawyer, legal-aid service, union or competent authority          |
| Debt, investments or major financial decisions | Regulated financial professional and primary institutional documents       |
| Employment disputes                            | Union, HR where appropriate, regulator, advocate or qualified professional |
| Safeguarding concerns                          | Relevant professional, authority or specialist service                     |
